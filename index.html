Privacy Policy

Lari – Leaving Checklist · Last updated: August 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Lari mobile application (the “App”). The App helps you put together a departure checklist before you leave.

1. Data We Collect

Lari does not collect any personal data. The App uses no analytics, shows no ads, requires no registration or account, and does not send any data to a server.

All your data stays on your device. Your lists, settings, and saved content are stored locally on your phone only.

2. Local Storage

Data stored on your device includes:

  • your checklists and the items/locations you add,
  • the App’s settings (theme, language),
  • photos you attach to checklist items. These photos are saved to the App’s document folder on your device and stay there until you delete them or delete the App. They are not uploaded anywhere.

This data is not accessible to any third party. If you delete the App or clear its data on your device, the data is removed from your phone.

Because everything is stored only on your device, your data is not backed up to any cloud service by Lari itself. To move your lists to a new phone, use the Export list option in the App’s menu and Import it on the other device — the transfer happens through the file you save, not through any server of ours. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all checklists and photos. Since all data stays on your device, you already have full access to it and can delete it at any time within the App.

3. Sharing a Checklist

When you share a checklist, the App builds a temporary image of it and hands it to the share menu of your phone (e.g. WhatsApp, e-mail). This temporary image is not stored permanently — it is removed after sharing. Whatever you send afterwards goes only through the app you chose; Lari does not transmit it.

4. Camera and Microphone Access

The App may ask to use the camera and the microphone:

  • the camera, to take a photo and attach it to a checklist item,
  • the microphone, to dictate voice-based tasks.

These permissions are used only with your in-the-moment consent.

Photos are saved locally and never uploaded by Lari.

Voice dictation uses your device’s built-in speech recognition service. Depending on your phone’s settings, that service may be provided by Google or your device manufacturer, and your speech may be processed on their servers under their own privacy policy. Lari does not record, store, or transmit audio itself. If you prefer not to use this, simply type your items instead.

5. Internet Connection

Lari works fully offline. The App itself does not send your checklists, photos, or settings anywhere.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at lariapp.support@gmail.com.

7. Children

Lari is not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from anyone, regardless of age.

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
