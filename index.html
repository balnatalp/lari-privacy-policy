Privacy Policy

Lari – Leaving Checklist · Last updated: August 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Lari mobile application (the “App”). The App helps you put together a departure checklist before you leave.

1. Data We Collect

Lari does not collect any personal data. The App uses no analytics, shows no ads, requires no registration or account, and does not send any data to a server.

All your data stays on your device. Your lists, settings, and saved content are stored locally on your phone only.

2. Local Storage

Data stored on your device includes:

  • your checklists and the items/locations you add,
  • the App’s settings (theme, language),
  • photos taken for sharing (the photo is not kept after sharing unless you save it yourself).

This data is not accessible to any third party. If you delete the App or clear its data on your device, the data is removed from your phone.

3. Camera and Microphone Access

The App may ask to use the camera and the microphone:

  • the camera, to take a photo for sharing,
  • the microphone, to dictate voice-based tasks.

These permissions are used only with your in-the-moment consent, and the App does not record or send out any audio or visual material.

4. Internet Connection

The App does not require an internet connection to work, and it does not transmit any data over the internet.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at lariapp.support@gmail.com.
